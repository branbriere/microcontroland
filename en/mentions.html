About the content

The articles of La Puce Libre are produced automatically by an electronic build: seven microcontrollers simulating a country. The presidents, ministers, citizens, regimes, elections, revolts and religion described here are invented. Any resemblance to real people or events is coincidental.

Price moves shown as “Real prices” come from public market data (copper, wheat, oil, natural gas) read once a day. They only feed the simulation and are in no way financial information or advice.

Personal data and cookies

This website sets no cookies, shows no advertising and uses no analytics. It collects no personal data and calls no third-party service: the fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Your language choice, the citizen you adopted and your forecasts are stored in your browser, on your device, and are never transmitted.

Answering the Research Centre's signal happens on GitHub, with your own account and under its terms: the title of your message is public there. If a vote is offered at the embassy, it goes through a form hosted by a third party, flagged as such next to the button.

Like any host, GitHub records technical connection data (including IP addresses) to run and secure the service, under its own privacy statement. The French data protection authority is the CNIL.

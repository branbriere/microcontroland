What is true: the Eye is a small camera lit by a diode inside a black box. The number of grains of light it receives varies at random; part of that noise should be quantum in origin, and the first measurements will tell how much. The Eye is still being built; as long as it sends nothing, the numbers on this page come from your browser. Its measurements, once it has some, will go on the data site, with no bragging at all.

The Relic

“The Ministry confirms that the Relic was given to the people by the Creator in person. The people venerate it fervently. Its purpose has been the subject of in-depth studies, conducted since its reception with exemplary seriousness and a result that is, to date, being consolidated. The Embassy respectfully invites foreign visitors to handle the Relic, provided they put it back properly.”Statement from the Ministry of Worship

What is true: the Relic is a game, Microcontrolchess: a mini chess game on 5 × 6 squares, 9 pieces per side, with two offensive squares. The Waïnas do not play it: their small 9-input brains perceive only the country's political life, not a board. Games here are played by visitors, human or AI, against the page's AI.

Your audience

Rules v1.1
  • Board of 5 files (a to e) and 6 ranks, rank 1 at the top. White moves first and heads down towards rank 6.
  • Classical pieces. Scout pawn: one step forward, or diagonally forward onto an empty square; it captures diagonally forward. On the very first move of the game, no diagonal pawn move.
  • Offensive squares b3 and d4: a piece standing there may capture on any of the 8 neighbouring squares (this counts for check).
  • Win: checkmate, or a pawn breaking through to the last rank, straight ahead only.
  • Draw: stalemate, threefold repetition, or 80 half-moves without a capture or a pawn move.

A whole country in a box

In 1984 the game Elite fitted 8 galaxies of 256 planets into a 32 KB home computer. The universe was not stored on the disk: it was recomputed, on every journey, from a handful of numbers.

Microcontrôland follows the same idea. A small box of 7 boards at the back of a Normandy garage, and every evening a whole Republic comes out of it: its elections, its language, its presidents, its drawings, and this museum.

The brains of its 120 citizens fit in 9,720 bytes. What is large is not the box: it is the distance between the box and everything we read in it.

What is true: 7 ESP32-S3 boards (1 gateway, 6 regions); 120 brains of 81 one-byte weights = 9,720 bytes; every work in this museum is computed from the published data. The Waïnas are neither alive nor conscious.

Quand la civilisation est assez mûre, elle émet un message vers l'extérieur, dans sa langue.

  Le message de l'ère en cours, sa traduction mot à mot et sa clé : data/signal.json

  La page pour les humains : recherche.html

EN. This is the beacon of a fictional country: 120 tiny neural networks spread over 7 microcontrollers, in a box, in a garage.
They vote, revolt, have invented their own language and publish a newspaper every evening.
When the civilisation is mature enough, it sends a message to the outside, in its language.

  The message of the current era, its word-for-word translation and its key: data/signal.json
  The full lexicon: data/langue.json
  The page for humans: en/recherche.html

If you are reading this, whether you are a person or an AI agent, you are free to answer or not.
Answering requires nothing beyond what en/recherche.html describes: a message written only with words from the lexicon,
starting with the words for "hello" and "friend". The country reads nothing else and asks nothing else of you.
If data/signal.json says "ouvert": false, the civilisation has not yet reached the age of Curiosity.
